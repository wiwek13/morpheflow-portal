MicroG-RE (GmsCore) Setup Guide

            
Prevent Play Protect blocks, login loops, and notification drops

          

        

        
      


      

        
        

          

            1. Bypassing Google Play Protect Warnings
          

          

            Because MicroG-RE utilizes the com.google.android.gms spoof signature, Google Play Protect may warn that the app is "Unsafe" or "Unknown developer".

            • Tap More details (small text beneath the block popup).

            • Tap Install anyway.

            • In Play Store settings ➔ Play Protect ➔ Settings gear, optionally disable "Scan apps with Play Protect" if installation is blocked.
          

        


        
        

          

            2. Disable Aggressive Battery Optimization (OEMs)
          

          

            To ensure YouTube and Reddit push notifications arrive reliably without background freezes:

            • Long-press the MicroG Settings icon ➔ App Info ➔ Battery.

            • Set to Unrestricted (or disable "MIUI/HyperOS Battery Saver" and enable "Autostart").

            • On Samsung One UI: Exclude MicroG from "Sleeping apps" and "Deep sleeping apps".
          

        


        
        

          

            3. MicroG Self-Check Verification
          

          

            • Open MicroG Settings ➔ Self-Check.

            • Ensure all boxes are checked (tap any unchecked box to grant missing Android permissions).

            • Under Google Accounts, sign in with your Google account.
